运营新招的实习生把手机号填成了邮箱、日期列混了三种格式，10 万条数据导进去 2 万条报错，排查到半夜。规则校验得自动化——配一次 rules.json，以后导入前先跑一遍。

`datavalid -input users.csv -rules rules.json`，rules.json 长这样：`{"rules":[{"column":"email","type":"string","required":true,"pattern":"^[\\w.-]+@[\\w.-]+\\.\\w+$"},{"column":"age","type":"int","min":0,"max":150},{"column":"status","type":"enum","values":["active","inactive","pending"]},{"column":"user_id","type":"string","unique":true}],"separator":","}`。type 支持 string/int/float/date/bool/enum 六种。逐行逐列校验：required 检查空值，pattern 正则匹配，range 检查 min/max，enum 检查值在列表内，unique 检查无重复。

输出到 stdout：`{"valid":false,"total":500,"valid_rows":487,"invalid_rows":13,"errors":[{"row":3,"column":"email","value":"@bad","message":"pattern mismatch"}]}`。`-report valid.csv` 和 `-report invalid.csv` 分别导出通过和不通过的行。`-format table` 终端表格。文件不存在退出码 1，规则格式错误退出码 2，全部通过退出码 0。

Go 标准库实现，不依赖第三方包。go build 能编译通过。
